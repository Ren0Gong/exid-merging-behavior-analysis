# Case case_03

## Case identity

Recording 54, track 419.

## Concise behavioral characterization

This case combines a strong, nearly continuous speed increase with an interaction concentrated mainly in the first seven seconds. PCAD rose abruptly from 0 into leftRear and leftAlongside episodes, reached two early peaks, and then became 0 for a long middle interval. The largest longitudinal and lateral jerk magnitudes also occurred during or close to the early PCAD episode. Speed continued to increase after that episode, while jerk changed sign and later settled; a small, separate lead interaction appeared near 14-15 s.

## Main temporal phases

1. **Initial acceleration without positive PCAD (about 0.00-1.76 s).** PCAD remained 0 with `No Risk`. Velocity increased from 14.81 to 15.94 m/s. Positive longitudinal jerk grew to 0.413 m/s³ and lateral jerk to 0.248 m/s³ by 1.76 s.

2. **First left-side interaction (about 1.80-2.72 s).** The critical source was initially `leftRear` and then `leftAlongside`. PCAD jumped to 0.919 m/s at 1.80 s, reached a local maximum of 1.324 m/s from leftAlongside at 2.48 s, and declined to 0.551 m/s at 2.72 s. Velocity continued rising from 15.98 to 16.95 m/s. Longitudinal jerk was about 0.42-0.44 m/s³, while lateral jerk rose from 0.255 to 0.413 m/s³.

3. **Second peak and interaction decay (about 2.76-6.84 s).** The source returned to `leftRear`. PCAD rose from 0.370 m/s at 2.76 s to the case maximum of 1.559 m/s at 3.32 s, remained above 0.5 m/s through 4.80 s, and then generally declined, with smaller local peaks of 0.376 m/s at 5.20 s and 0.449 m/s at 6.76 s. Velocity rose strongly from 17.00 to 24.62 m/s. Positive jerk peaked early—lateral jerk reached 0.470 m/s³ at 3.48 s—then longitudinal jerk became negative and reached -0.290 m/s³ at 6.84 s.

4. **Long zero-PCAD speed increase (about 6.88-13.84 s).** PCAD was 0 with `No Risk` throughout. Velocity increased from 24.69 to 30.19 m/s. Longitudinal jerk remained negative through much of the first half of this phase, reaching the case minimum of -0.303 m/s³ at 9.56 s, before returning toward zero and becoming modestly positive. Lateral jerk was small and briefly negative around 9-10 s.

5. **Small lead interaction and final resolution (about 13.88-16.24 s).** A `lead` interaction developed from 13.88 to 15.12 s, with PCAD rising only to 0.259 m/s. Velocity increased from 30.23 to 31.31 m/s during it. PCAD then returned to 0 with `No Risk` from 15.16 s through the end, while velocity reached 32.25 m/s and both jerk components remained near zero.

## Safety interpretation

The meaningful PCAD activity was concentrated in two connected early left-side episodes. PCAD was at or above 0.5 m/s from 1.80 to 2.72 s and again from 2.88 to 4.80 s. The first episode changed from leftRear to leftAlongside and peaked at 1.324 m/s at 2.48 s. The second, larger leftRear episode peaked at 1.559 m/s at 3.32 s and then decayed over several seconds.

After 6.84 s, PCAD dropped to 0 for nearly seven seconds. The later lead interaction was much smaller, peaking at 0.259 m/s at 15.12 s, and was followed by another zero-PCAD interval. Thus, the largest modeled interaction difficulty was early, repeated across two peaks, and followed by a long recovery rather than sustained across the full record.

## Efficiency interpretation

Velocity increased from the case minimum of 14.810 m/s at 0.00 s to the maximum of 32.249 m/s at 16.24 s. The increase continued through both early PCAD peaks and was especially strong from about 3 to 7 s. It then continued at a lower rate through the long zero-PCAD interval and strengthened modestly again after approximately 11 s.

There was no observed speed reversal during the record. Negative longitudinal jerk from roughly 5.5 to 10.5 s coincided with continued velocity growth, indicating a change in the evolution of acceleration rather than deceleration of the vehicle itself. No external benchmark is available for judging the absolute speed level or efficiency.

## Comfort interpretation

The dominant comfort-related disturbance was early and overlapped the PCAD episode. Longitudinal jerk reached a maximum of 0.438 m/s³ at about 2.36-2.48 s, while lateral jerk continued upward to a maximum of 0.470 m/s³ at 3.48 s. Both components were therefore large in absolute magnitude near the two PCAD peaks, with lateral jerk slightly more prominent at its maximum.

As PCAD decayed, longitudinal jerk crossed through zero and became negative, reaching -0.303 m/s³ at 9.56 s during a zero-PCAD interval. Lateral jerk had already fallen to 0.050 m/s³ by 6.84 s and later reached only -0.085 m/s³ at its minimum. From about 11 s onward, jerk magnitudes were much smaller, and they were close to zero during the small late lead interaction and final zero-PCAD phase. The motion therefore became smoother after the early disturbance, despite a later interval of sustained negative longitudinal jerk.

## Cross-dimensional relationships

The onset of positive PCAD at 1.80 s coincided with strong positive longitudinal and rising lateral jerk, while velocity was already increasing. The highest PCAD at 3.32 s was followed closely by the lateral-jerk maximum at 3.48 s, and velocity continued its steep rise. As PCAD decayed toward 0, longitudinal jerk became negative, yet speed still increased. The long middle interval combined PCAD 0 with rising speed and gradually stabilizing jerk. These temporal associations are direct observations, but they do not establish that the interaction caused the jerk or velocity changes.

## Strongest quantitative evidence

- PCAD was 0 with `No Risk` from 0.00 through 1.76 s.
- PCAD reached 1.324 m/s from `leftAlongside` at 2.48 s and the case maximum of 1.559 m/s from `leftRear` at 3.32 s.
- PCAD was at least 0.5 m/s during two early intervals: approximately 1.80-2.72 s and 2.88-4.80 s.
- PCAD was 0 continuously from 6.88 through 13.84 s; the later `lead` episode peaked at only 0.259 m/s at 15.12 s.
- Velocity increased from 14.810 m/s at 0.00 s to 32.249 m/s at 16.24 s.
- Jerk extrema were -0.303 to 0.438 m/s³ longitudinally and -0.085 to 0.470 m/s³ laterally. The positive longitudinal maximum occurred near 2.36-2.48 s, and the lateral maximum occurred at 3.48 s.

## Uncertain or weakly supported inferences

The record does not identify driver intent, lane-change start, lane crossing, merge completion, or the reason for the early left-side interactions. It provides no surrounding-vehicle speeds, gaps, road geometry, speed limit, or desired speed. PCAD peaks do not establish imminent collision or a specific evasive manoeuvre. Although jerk and PCAD peaks overlap, causality cannot be inferred. The case cannot be assigned to a cluster or predefined behavioral type from these variables.

## Overall case interpretation

Directly observed, the vehicle accelerated throughout the record while PCAD was concentrated in an early sequence involving leftRear and leftAlongside vehicles. Two early peaks, the larger being 1.559 m/s at 3.32 s, coincided with the case's strongest positive jerk activity; PCAD then decayed to 0 and remained there for most of the remaining record. Speed continued upward during and after the interaction, while jerk first turned negative and then stabilized. A cautious interpretation is that the most dynamically demanding portion occurred early and was followed by sustained progression with little modeled interaction, but neither the driver's intent nor the exact merging stage is available.
