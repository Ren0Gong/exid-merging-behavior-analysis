# Case case_02

## Case identity

Recording 18, track 67.

## Concise behavioral characterization

The vehicle accelerated strongly through the first two-thirds of the record, while PCAD developed from no modeled interaction to a prolonged leftRear escalation with a sharp maximum at 10.64 s. Velocity continued to increase during that escalation, but its growth weakened and later changed to a gradual decline. Jerk disturbances were modest in absolute size and were most clearly negative during the rear-related peak near 4 s and the leftRear escalation near 10-11 s. Later interaction sources varied, including a one-frame leftAlongside spike, before PCAD returned to 0.

## Main temporal phases

1. **Initial acceleration without positive PCAD (about 0.00-1.04 s).** PCAD was 0 with `No Risk`. Velocity rose from 15.85 to 16.62 m/s. Positive longitudinal and lateral jerk developed, reaching 0.078 and 0.118 m/s³ at 1.04 s.

2. **Rear interaction with continued speed gain (about 1.08-7.20 s).** `rear` remained the critical source. PCAD varied upward to a local maximum of 0.593 m/s at 3.96 s and then declined toward 0.30 m/s by 7.20 s. Velocity increased from 16.65 to 21.30 m/s. The strongest early lateral jerk was 0.198 m/s³ at 1.80 s; around 3.9-4.1 s, longitudinal jerk reached approximately -0.183 m/s³ and lateral jerk about -0.105 m/s³.

3. **Sustained leftRear escalation (about 7.24-11.84 s).** The critical source changed to `leftRear`. PCAD crossed 1.0 m/s at 8.64 s, exceeded 2 m/s from about 9.96 to 10.64 s, and reached the case maximum of 3.227 m/s at 10.64 s. It then dropped but remained near 1.35 m/s through 11.84 s. Velocity still rose from 21.32 to 23.52 m/s, although its increase became progressively smaller. Longitudinal and lateral jerk were negative during the steepest PCAD rise, around -0.18 and -0.14 m/s³ at their largest magnitudes in this phase.

4. **Lower PCAD and velocity plateau (about 11.88-15.40 s).** The source changed to `rightRear` and then `rear`. PCAD stepped down to 0.504 m/s at 11.88 s and generally declined into the 0.26-0.39 m/s range. Velocity reached its case maximum of 23.550 m/s at 12.84 s and then eased to 23.30 m/s by 15.40 s. Jerk magnitudes also declined, with longitudinal jerk approaching zero and lateral jerk becoming modestly positive.

5. **Mixed late interactions and resolution (about 15.44-19.28 s).** The source alternated among leftRear, a single `leftAlongside` frame at 16.20 s, rear, and leftRear. PCAD was mostly below 0.6 m/s, except for the one-frame 1.333 m/s leftAlongside value and a late leftRear peak of 0.711 m/s at 18.88 s. Velocity declined gradually to about 22.78 m/s and then stabilized. PCAD became 0 with `No Risk` from 18.92 s to the end, and both jerk components ended at 0.

## Safety interpretation

The principal safety-relevant pattern is the multi-second leftRear escalation. After a moderate rear interaction, PCAD was at or above 0.5 m/s from approximately 7.76 to 11.88 s and at or above 1.0 m/s from 8.64 through 11.84 s. Its sharp maximum of 3.227 m/s at 10.64 s was brief, but it occurred within a sustained period of elevated leftRear PCAD rather than in isolation. Values immediately after the maximum remained around 1.35 m/s until 11.84 s.

Later PCAD was lower and more fragmented. The 1.333 m/s value at 16.20 s came from `leftAlongside` for only one observed frame; surrounding frames reverted to leftRear values below 0.4 m/s. A separate rise culminated at 0.711 m/s from leftRear at 18.88 s, followed immediately by `No Risk` and PCAD 0. These late events are therefore brief compared with the 7.24-11.84 s leftRear episode.

## Efficiency interpretation

Velocity rose from 15.850 m/s at the start to 23.550 m/s at 12.84 s. The increase was strongest early and continued during the main PCAD escalation, but became progressively flatter near 10-13 s. After its maximum, speed declined gradually to 22.768 m/s around 18.00 s and ended nearly stable at 22.784 m/s.

The strongest leftRear PCAD rise did not coincide with a speed decrease; speed increased from 22.25 m/s at the first 1.0 m/s PCAD observation (8.64 s) to 23.28 m/s at the 10.64 s maximum. The later speed decline occurred while PCAD was mostly lower and mixed in source. This timing describes the observed progression only; no external efficiency benchmark is available.

## Comfort interpretation

Jerk was not dominated by a single isolated extreme. Lateral jerk reached its positive maximum of 0.198 m/s³ at 1.80 s during the early rear interaction. Longitudinal jerk reached its most negative value, -0.183 m/s³, at 3.92 s and again near 10.20 s; the first occurrence accompanied the rear PCAD plateau near 0.59 m/s, and the second occurred during the leftRear escalation. Lateral jerk reached -0.140 m/s³ at 10.28 s.

Negative longitudinal and lateral jerk persisted through the main PCAD peak and early recovery. By about 13-15 s, longitudinal jerk was closer to zero and lateral jerk was small and positive. During the late mixed interactions, both components remained modest and settled to approximately zero by 19.28 s. Thus, the most coherent comfort disturbance coincided with the main leftRear escalation, while the one-frame 16.20 s PCAD spike did not coincide with an unusually large jerk value.

## Cross-dimensional relationships

The rear PCAD maximum near 3.96 s coincided with negative longitudinal and lateral jerk, even though velocity continued rising. A similar but longer association appears from roughly 9.5 to 11.8 s: leftRear PCAD rose sharply while both jerk components were negative and the speed increase flattened. The vehicle's actual speed maximum came later, at 12.84 s, after PCAD had stepped down and the source had changed. The late speed decline accompanied smaller and intermittent PCAD values rather than the case maximum. These associations do not establish that PCAD changes caused the jerk or speed evolution.

## Strongest quantitative evidence

- PCAD was 0 with `No Risk` from 0.00 through 1.04 s.
- The early `rear` episode peaked at 0.593 m/s at 3.96 s.
- During the `leftRear` episode, PCAD was at least 0.5 m/s for about 4.16 s (7.76-11.88 s) and at least 1.0 m/s for about 3.24 s (8.64-11.84 s).
- PCAD reached 3.227 m/s from `leftRear` at 10.64 s; velocity was 23.278 m/s and jerk was -0.175 m/s³ longitudinally and -0.133 m/s³ laterally.
- Velocity increased from 15.850 m/s at 0.00 s to a maximum of 23.550 m/s at 12.84 s, then ended at 22.784 m/s.
- The one-frame `leftAlongside` value was 1.333 m/s at 16.20 s; the late leftRear peak was 0.711 m/s at 18.88 s, followed by PCAD 0 at 18.92 s.
- Observed jerk extrema were -0.183 to 0.115 m/s³ longitudinally and -0.140 to 0.198 m/s³ laterally.

## Uncertain or weakly supported inferences

The data do not establish the driver's intent, exact lane-change or merge timing, surrounding-vehicle speeds, available gaps, road geometry, speed-limit compliance, or a desired speed. A rise in PCAD does not prove imminent collision or a particular evasive action. The single leftAlongside frame at 16.20 s is too brief to support a sustained-state interpretation. No causal relation among PCAD, speed, and jerk, and no cluster membership, can be established from the supplied variables.

## Overall case interpretation

Directly observed, the case moved from no positive PCAD into a moderate rear interaction and then a sustained leftRear escalation whose 3.227 m/s maximum was sharp but embedded in more than three seconds of PCAD above 1 m/s. Speed continued to rise through that episode, peaked shortly afterward, and then declined gently. Negative jerk accompanied the strongest escalation, while later interactions were more fragmented and motion settled toward zero jerk as PCAD returned to 0. A cautious interpretation is that the manoeuvre progressed through one dominant leftRear interaction followed by lower, changing interactions, but the record cannot reveal the driver's strategy or whether any speed or motion change was a deliberate response.
